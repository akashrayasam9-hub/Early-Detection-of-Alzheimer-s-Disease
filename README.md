# Early-Detection-of-Alzheimer-s-Disease
Early Detection of Alzheimer’s Disease Using MRI Images and Transfer Learning (ML)

**Business Problem**
Alzheimer’s Disease (AD) is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder that impacts cognitive
abilities, leading to memory loss, confusion, and ultimately, the inability to perform daily
tasks. Early detection of Alzheimer’s Disease is critical for effective intervention and
management. This project seeks to answer the question:
Can MRI image analysis through transfer learning models accurately classify patients in
various stages of Alzheimer’s Disease to support timely diagnosis and intervention?
The goal is to develop an automated system that can classify MRI scans into distinct stages
of Alzheimer’s Disease—Mild Demented (MD), Moderate Demented (MoD), Non-Demented
(ND), and Very Mild Demented (VMD). By leveraging MRI data, the system aims to assist
healthcare professionals in identifying patients in the early stages of Alzheimer's, allowing
for timely treatment and better management of the disease.
